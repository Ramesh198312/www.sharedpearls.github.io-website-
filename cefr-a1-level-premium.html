Your foundation journey

Eight stages from first words to Ready for B1

Each stage builds on the one before it. Open a stage to see what you learn and what you can do at the end of it.

“Where am I going?”

  1. STAGE 01A1

    Starting English

    Letters, numbers, greetings and your first sentences with be.

  2. STAGE 02A1

    Essential Communication

    Personal information, family, pronouns and simple questions.

  3. STAGE 03A1

    Building Foundations

    Present simple, routines, articles, plurals and core vocabulary.

  4. STAGE 04A1

    Everyday English

    Shopping, food, directions, can, and talking about now.

  5. STAGE 05A2

    Expanding Communication

    Past simple, past continuous, comparison and richer vocabulary.

  6. STAGE 06A2

    Using English in Real Life

    Future plans, present perfect, advice, travel, work and health.

  7. STAGE 07A2

    A2 Consolidation

    Bring the four skills together and review everything you know.

  8. STAGE 08Next

    Ready for B1

    Check your foundation and prepare for more independent English.

How every lesson works

Learn → Practise → Use → Review

The same simple rhythm in every part of the course, so you always know what to do.

Step 1

Learn

Understand the language with clear, short explanations.

Step 2

Practise

Use it in controlled activities until it feels comfortable.

Step 3

Use

Apply it in real communication — speaking and writing.

Step 4

Review

Come back, check what you remember and make it stronger.

A1 foundation pathway

Your first four stages

Follow this order if you are completely new to English. Each step has a small, clear goal.

Filter the catalogue to A1
  1. 01
    Starting English — letters, numbers, first words

    Say and spell your name, count, read simple signs and greet people.

    Alphabet Numbers Greetings Be: am / is / are
  2. 02
    Essential Communication — people and personal information

    Introduce yourself, talk about family, and ask simple questions.

    Personal information Subject pronouns Family Question words
  3. 03
    Building Foundations — routines and everyday facts

    Talk about what you do every day, where things are, and what you have.

    Present simple Frequency adverbs Daily routines Have got
  4. 04
    Everyday English — shops, food, places and now

    Buy things, order food, find your way and say what is happening now.

    Present continuous Can At a shop Asking for directions

What you'll learn to do

By the end of this course you can…

Simple, practical things you will be able to do in English.

  • introduce yourself and spell your name
  • talk about your family and friends
  • describe your home and where you live
  • ask and answer common everyday questions
  • understand simple signs, notices and messages
  • shop, order food and ask the price
  • talk about your daily routine and free time
  • describe past events and tell a short story
  • talk about plans and arrangements
  • write short everyday messages and emails
  • follow simple conversations about familiar things
  • ask for help, repetition and directions
  • handle common travel situations
  • give simple opinions and reasons
  • communicate comfortably in familiar situations
