Know what your exam asks you to write

Choose the exam you are preparing for. Identify the response type, time limit and length before planning. Use the task briefing below each prompt to decide how to organise your answer.

IELTS

60 minutes total. Complete both tasks. Allow about 20 minutes for Task 1 and 40 minutes for Task 2. Task 2 carries twice the weight.

  • Academic Task 1. At least 150 words describing a chart, table, diagram or process. Summarise the main features, give an overview and support it with selected comparisons or stages.
  • General Training Task 1. At least 150 words in letter form. Cover the three bullet points, make your purpose clear and choose a tone that fits the recipient.
  • Task 2 in both versions. At least 250 words. Answer every part of the question, make your position clear where required and develop relevant reasons or examples.

Before submitting. Write connected prose. Review task coverage, coherence and cohesion, vocabulary, and grammatical range and accuracy. Keep interpretations of data within what the source shows.

TOEFL iBT

ETS estimates about 23 minutes for 12 computer-based Writing items in the current format. Follow the instructions and timer displayed for each item.

  • Build a Sentence. Arrange the given words or phrases into a complete, grammatical statement or question.
  • Write an Email. Respond to the situation and complete the requested actions. Give the recipient the information they need and use a suitable tone.
  • Write for an Academic Discussion. Add a supported viewpoint to a classroom discussion. Engage with a classmate’s idea where it helps you make a distinct contribution.

Before submitting. Check word order in sentence items, practical details in the email, and development of your own idea in the discussion. Word ranges shown in this lab are practice targets.

Linguaskill

The current Writing module lasts 55 minutes for two tasks. Cambridge weights Part 1 at 25% and Part 2 at 75%.

  • Part 1 in both versions. Write an email. Cambridge recommends at least 50 words. Respond to the situation and every instruction, and match the tone to the recipient.
  • Linguaskill Part 2. Write an essay. Cambridge recommends at least 250 words. Develop a clear answer with relevant reasons or examples.
  • Linguaskill Business Part 2. Write a report. Cambridge recommends at least 250 words. Organise findings clearly and provide recommendations when the prompt asks for them.

Before submitting. For the email, check that the reader can act on your message. For the longer task, check that each paragraph or section serves the main purpose and that recommendations follow from the findings.

PTE Academic

The two writing task types covered here each have their own timer and response format.

  • Summarize Written Text. 10 minutes. Read the passage and write one complete sentence of 5–75 words. Preserve the central message and essential relationships.
  • Write Essay. 20 minutes. Write a 200–300-word argumentative essay, with a clear position and relevant development in paragraphs.

Before submitting. Check sentence form and source accuracy in the summary. Check length, task coverage, paragraph structure and language accuracy in the essay.

Using this lab. Foundation and skills drills may use shorter responses or more practice time. Use the target beside your prompt for that activity. The exam requirements above apply on test day. Checklists and the quiz guide practice and do not predict an exam score. Official sources

Check your writing knowledge · 12 questions

Choose one answer per question. You’ll get an overall score, a breakdown by exam and explanations to help you decide what to practise.

1. IELTS

How much weight does IELTS Writing Task 2 carry compared with Task 1?

Answer. Twice the weight. Task 2 contributes twice as much, so reserve roughly 40 of the 60 minutes for it.

2. TOEFL iBT

What is your job in Build a Sentence?

Answer. Arrange the given groups into a grammatical sentence or question. Build a Sentence tests sentence structure using the words or phrases supplied.

3. Linguaskill

Which pair matches the recommended minimum lengths in the current format?

Answer. Email 50 words; essay or Business report 250 words. Cambridge recommends a minimum of 50 words for Part 1 and 250 for Part 2.

4. PTE Academic

Which response meets the basic form for Summarize Written Text?

Answer. One complete sentence of 40 words. Pearson specifies one complete sentence of 5–75 words.

5. IELTS

You have 20 minutes for an Academic Task 1 chart. What should your answer contain?

Answer. An overview and selected details that support the main comparisons. A strong response identifies the main patterns and supports them with accurate data. Explanations of causes are outside the task unless the visual provides them.

6. TOEFL iBT

What adds the most value to an Academic Discussion response?

Answer. Add a supported point that engages with the question. The discussion asks you to contribute a developed idea of your own. A relevant example or line of reasoning helps.

7. Linguaskill

Which longer task is specific to Linguaskill Business in the current format?

Answer. A report. Both versions have an email first, while Business uses a report in Part 2 and general Linguaskill uses an essay.

8. PTE Academic

Which plan fits Write Essay?

Answer. Write 200–300 words organised around a position and supporting reasons. The specified length is 200–300 words. Use paragraphs to develop the position with relevant support.

9. IELTS

Car use fell from 58% to 45%. Which wording is precise?

Answer. Car use fell by 13 percentage points. Percentage points give the absolute difference between two percentages, 58 minus 45.

10. TOEFL iBT

Two classmates debate recorded lectures. What develops a new contribution?

Answer. Propose recording explanations while keeping live group work, and illustrate the benefit. A concrete proposal and example extend the conversation instead of repeating it.

11. PTE Academic

A pilot reduced delivery stops at convenient locker sites but sometimes increased car trips elsewhere. What must the summary retain?

Answer. The contrast and the role of location. The change varied by location. Losing that condition would alter the source’s conclusion.

12. Linguaskill

Which sentence makes a professional request actionable?

Answer. Could we run a thirty-minute online replacement on Monday at six? The first option specifies an action, a format, a duration and a time.

What makes it work

Editing checklist for any task
  • Task coverage — Underline every instruction. Mark the sentence or paragraph where you address each point.
  • Organisation — Give each paragraph a job. Check that the reader can follow your reason, example and conclusion.
  • Language choice — Check that words fit the topic and the relationship with your reader. Replace a vague word with a precise one.
  • Accuracy — Read your revision slowly for sentence boundaries, agreement, verb forms, spelling and punctuation.
