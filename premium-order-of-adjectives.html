    Course description

    You will move from the basic positions of adjectives (before a noun, after a linking verb, after a noun or pronoun) to the conventional order of adjectives, and then to the finer points that separate competent from expert users: classifying modifiers, meaning shifts such as the present situation and the students present, and dense academic noun phrases.

    The adjective-order framework is taught as a strong convention, not an absolute rule. You will learn when meaning, context, coordination and style lead proficient writers to choose differently.

    Learning outcomes

    • Sequence adjectives naturally before a noun.
    • Use attributive, predicative and postpositive adjectives accurately.
    • Explain how position changes meaning.
    • Analyse and write precise academic noun phrases.
    • Distinguish grammaticality, naturalness and style.
