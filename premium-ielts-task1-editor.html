03. Vocabulary: Adjectives & Adverbs for Trends

Master subjective vs. objective vocabulary and grammar shifts.

Task 1 Adverb Filter

Task 1 requires objective reporting. Identify appropriate vs. inappropriate adverbs:

✅ Steadily
❌ Shockingly (Subjective)
✅ Gradually
❌ Amazingly (Subjective)
✅ Sharply
❌ Predictably (Subjective)

Structure Shift: (Verb + Adverb) ➔ (Adjective + Noun)

Increase Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA) by varying sentence structures:

Verb + Adverb:

"Traffic rose steadily between 1990 and 2000."

Transformed (Adjective + Noun):

"Traffic saw a steady rise between 1990 and 2000."

Verb + Adverb:

"Lifestyle reasons grew consistently across the period."

Transformed (There was a...):

"There was a consistent growth in numbers moving for lifestyle reasons."

01. Task 2 Architecture: 4-Paragraph Formula

Master paragraph cohesion and explicit thesis statements for Problem/Solution prompts.

Paragraph 1

Introduction

Paraphrase prompt + State thesis outlining primary causes and solutions.

Paragraph 2

Body 1: Causes

Topic sentence + Explain primary cause (e.g., emissions) + Secondary cause + Example.

Paragraph 3

Body 2: Solutions

Topic sentence + Governmental action + Infrastructure investment + Projected outcome.

Paragraph 4

Conclusion

Summarize main causes and reinforce the viability of proposed solutions.

02. Lexical Resource: High-Impact Urban Collocations
