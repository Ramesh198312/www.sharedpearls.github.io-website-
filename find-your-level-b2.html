Before you start

  • You have 3 hours in total: Listening 40 min, Reading 60 min, Writing 60 min and Speaking 20 min.
  • Each module has its own timer. It starts when you press Start the test and runs only while you are working in that module. It pauses when you go to another module and stops when you leave or close this page. If you refresh or come back later, each timer carries on from the time already used (your answers are kept).
  • To start the whole test again, use Reset and retake the test at the bottom of the page.
  • When a module’s time is over you will see a reminder. The test does not close, so you can still finish.
  • Each module is divided into parts. Use the part buttons, or the question numbers at the bottom of the screen, to move around. Your answers are saved in this browser.
  • In Listening you can hear each recording once, as in the real exam. You can pause it. Each recording has silent reading time before each set of questions (30 to 45 seconds) and checking time at the end, with a countdown on the player.
  • The Speaking module needs a microphone. Recording starts automatically after a tone. Your recordings stay on this device and are not uploaded.
  • You can submit only when all four modules are complete.

Listening

Four parts, 40 questions. You hear each recording once. Each one gives you time to read the questions before you listen and to check your answers at the end.

Reading

Eight parts, 40 questions. Read each text carefully and answer every question.

Writing

Three tasks. Task 1 is marked automatically. Tasks 2 and 3 are scored on a 0–5 scale when you submit them.

Speaking

Six task types, 15 recordings. Press Start on each item: preparation time, a tone, then recording begin automatically.
