Advanced English
Level Test

A demanding, international-exam-style test of listening, reading, academic writing and spoken English. Every text and recording is original. When you finish, you receive a detailed report with your CEFR level, a score for each module and targeted advice.

  1. 01 · 40 min

    Listening

    Four sections: a phone call about gym membership, a professor’s talk with a site map, two students discussing fieldwork and a science lecture. Each recording is played once, with time to read the questions.

  2. 02 · 60 min

    Reading

    Seven parts, from gap-fills and a workplace memo to two long academic passages, with fourteen different question types.

  3. 03 · 60 min

    Writing

    Report on a line graph (150+ words) and write a discussion essay (250+ words). Each task receives a band from 0 to 9 on four criteria.

  4. 04 · 20 min

    Speaking

    A four-question interview, then four timed tasks that combine reading, listening and speaking. Every answer is recorded and saved for you to play back.

Before you start

  • You have 3 hours in total: Listening 40 min, Reading 60 min, Writing 60 min and Speaking 20 min.
  • Each module has its own timer. It starts when you press Start the test and runs only while you are working in that module. It pauses when you go to another module and stops when you leave or close this page. If you refresh or come back later, each timer carries on from the time already used, and your answers are kept.
  • To start the whole test again, use Reset and retake the test at the bottom of the page.
  • When a module’s time is over, you will see a reminder. The test does not close, so you can still finish.
  • Use the Navigator to move between modules, parts and questions. Flag any question you want to come back to, and use Review to see what is unanswered or flagged.
  • In Reading, select words in a passage to highlight them. Tap a highlight to remove it.
  • In Listening you hear each recording once. Each section includes time to read the questions. You can pause, but you cannot rewind.
  • The Speaking module needs a microphone. Recording starts automatically after a tone. Your recordings stay on this device and are not uploaded.
  • You can submit only when all four modules are complete.
