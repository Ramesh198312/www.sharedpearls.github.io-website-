Task Achievement rewards selection and comparison, not transcription. Click each stage to see what belongs in it and what quietly damages the score.

Description or interpretation?

Overview (interpretation): Overall, car ownership rose across all four countries, with the sharpest growth in Country B, which overtook Country A after 2010.

Detail masquerading as an overview: In 1990 Country A was 25%, in 1995 it was 31%, in 2000 it was 34%…

Interpretation means identifying the pattern that holds the data together. It does not mean explaining why the change happened — Task 1 gives you no evidence for causes.

Tense, register and accuracy

Past data: Between 1990 and 2010, consumption rose by roughly a third.

Projected data: Consumption is expected to plateau after 2030.

Timeless process: The pulp is then filtered before being pressed into sheets.

Map, past to present: The woodland to the north was cleared and replaced by housing.

A single wrong figure costs more than an unusual adjective. Check every number you copy from the visual.
