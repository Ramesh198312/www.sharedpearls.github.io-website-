Linguistic Foundation

The Grammar Framework Underpinning Every Lesson

We teach nominalization as functional grammar in context rather than treating every derivative as interchangeable.

Construction Example Teaching Focus & Linguistic Function
Deverbal Noun decide → decision A noun derived morphologically from a verb base with specific suffixation.
Deadjectival Noun accurate → accuracy An abstract state or quality derived from an adjectival property.
Zero Conversion (Functional Shift) to increase → an increase Grammatical category change without overt affixation; stress shift in speech.
Gerund-Participial Clause Reading improves focus. Clause functioning in nominal role; distinct from fully nominal "the reading of the text".
Process vs. Participant Distinction teach → teaching vs teacher -er forms denote human participants/agents, not the nominalized event process itself.
Clausal & Grammatical Metaphor Prices rose → The price rise Packing full propositions into noun phrases for thematic progression.
