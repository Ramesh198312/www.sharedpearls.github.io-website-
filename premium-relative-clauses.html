Relative Clauses

The grammar of adding information without starting a new sentence.

This laboratory takes you from the first question a relative clause answers — which one? — to the structures that decide how formal, how clear and how natural your English sounds. You read a rule, see it working, take it apart on screen, and then prove you can use it.

How to read the badges

A Source badge marks a rule taken from the reference grammar supplied for this module. An Editorial badge marks explanation, examples or contrast added by us from standard British and American grammar references.
