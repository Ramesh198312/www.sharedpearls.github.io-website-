CEFR placement · B1 Intermediate

Intermediate English Level Test

Four modules in the styles of IELTS, PTE, TOEFL and Linguaskill, built around original news, science and feature texts. Read, listen, write and speak, then get your score for each module, your CEFR band and clear advice on what to practise next.

Reading

Notices, an email, and news and science articles on sleep, cities, wildlife and work.

40 questions · about 60 min

Listening

Conversations, a briefing, an interview and short talks by British, Australian and Canadian speakers.

27 questions · about 40 min

Writing

An email reply and an opinion essay, each scored with a strict four-part rubric.

2 tasks · about 60 min

Speaking

Interview, long turn and discussion on one topic. Record, listen back and check yourself.

3 recordings · about 20 min

Before you start

  • You have 3 hours in total: Reading 60 min, Listening 40 min, Writing 60 min and Speaking 20 min.
  • Each module has its own timer. It starts when you press Start the test and runs only while you are working in that module. It pauses when you go to another module and stops when you leave or close this page. If you refresh or come back later, each timer carries on from the time already used (your answers are kept).
  • To start the whole test again, use Reset and retake the test at the bottom of the page.
  • When a module’s time is over you will see a reminder. The test does not close, so you can still finish.
  • You can move between modules in any order. Your answers are saved in this browser.
  • You can play each listening recording twice. Use headphones if you can.
  • The Speaking module needs a microphone. Your recordings stay on this device and are not uploaded.
  • You can submit only when all four modules are complete: every question answered, both writing tasks scored and the speaking tasks done.

When every module is complete, submit the test to see your report.
