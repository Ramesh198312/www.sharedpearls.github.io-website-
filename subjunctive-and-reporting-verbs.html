▤A / The object test

Who is allowed to move?

If a person comes directly after the verb, you are probably in advise territory: She advised me to wait. Suggest and recommend need a different bridge.

?B / The tone test

How much room is left?

Suggest leaves the door open. Recommend adds informed confidence. Insist quietly closes the door.
