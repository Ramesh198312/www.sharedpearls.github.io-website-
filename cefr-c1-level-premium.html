A purposeful route through the level. Work through the stages in order, or move directly to whatever your current study, work or examination demands.

    Consolidate Advanced Control

    Strengthen the grammatical and lexical control you brought from B2 so that accuracy holds under pressure.

    Advanced grammar
  2. 02

    Develop Precision

    Express fine distinctions accurately through careful word choice, collocation and structure.

    Vocabulary & lexis
  3. 03

    Build Nuance

    Use grammar, vocabulary, tone and emphasis with greater subtlety and awareness of connotation.

    Word choice
  4. 04

    Control Extended Discourse

  5. 05

  6. 06

C1 in practice

What You Are Working Towards

Advanced control described in plain language. These statements describe a direction of travel, not a finished state.

  • Follow demanding, extended texts and recognise meaning that is implied rather than stated.
  • Express ideas fluently and spontaneously without obviously searching for words.
  • Use language flexibly across social, academic and professional situations.
  • Produce clear, well-structured and detailed writing on complex subjects.
  • Develop an argument across a long text, supporting claims with evidence.
  • Adapt register and tone deliberately for a particular audience and purpose.
  • Interpret a writer or speaker's stance, attitude and rhetorical intention.
  • Communicate nuanced distinctions of meaning with precision.
  • Take a confident part in demanding discussions, seminars and negotiations.
  • Use organisational patterns, connectors and cohesive devices with control.

Bring it together

C1 Integrated Communication Project

One extended piece of work that draws on every strand of the level: you investigate a question, read critically, synthesise sources, write it up, present it and then discuss and reflect on the choices you made. It is the clearest evidence of advanced control you can produce.

Open the project brief

01Frame a research question and define your audience.
02Read critically and evaluate the quality of your sources.
03Synthesise findings into a clear, hedged argument.
04Write the report, controlling register and cohesion.
05Present it, then handle questions and disagreement.
06Reflect on your language choices and next priorities.
