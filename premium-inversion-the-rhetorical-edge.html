Chapter 01 · The Power Move

Negative Inversion for Emphasis

Negative Adv + Aux + Subject + Verb → Never / Rarely / Seldom / Little + Aux + Subj

Never have I encountered such compelling evidence in this field.

Nature · Research Article · Band 8+ model

Rarely does the data support such a definitive conclusion.

Seldom has the academic community witnessed such a rapid paradigm shift.

Syntax Tree Emphasis Engine

Inversion is not decoration — it is re-wiring the syntax tree to put Focus first. Standard SVO buries emphasis. Inverted OSV / Adv-Aux-S-V moves the Emphasis node to the root-left, forcing the examiner to feel your stance.

S — Sentence root. All inversion starts here.
CP → Focus + Aux + Subj triggers C-head movement.
NP [Neg Adv] — Fronted negative adverbial phrase.
This is your Emphasis Engine: Never / Rarely / Seldom / Little. Moves to Spec-CP.
VP [Inverted] — The inverted verb phrase.
After fronting, the rest of the clause stays, but Aux-Subj order flips.
Det: Never — Determiner slot re-used as Negative Adverb.
Band 8+ trick: Never is not just vocab, it's a syntactic trigger.
Aux: have — Auxiliary jumps BEFORE subject.
Standard: I have → Inverted: have I. This swap = C1 grammar score.
NP: I — Subject now post-aux.
Pronoun / noun follows Aux. Creates dramatic pause and focus on Never.
VP: seen... — Main verb phrase remainder.
Past participle / complement. In essay: Never have I seen such compelling...

How to read the tree: Start at the top (CP). The left-most daughter is your Emphasis. Under Negative Inversion, that slot is filled by Never / Rarely / Seldom / Little / Not only / Under no circumstances. The rule forces Inverted order: Aux jumps before Subject. That jump is what examiners reward as C1-C2 control.

Chapter 06 · Scoring

Band 8+ Strategy When to Use

✓

Frequency: 2 inversions max per essay. One in introduction (Never has...), one in conclusion (Not only...). More looks forced.

★

Speaking Power Move: Start Speaking Part 3 answer with "Not only do I think X, but I also believe Y" = instant Band 8+ for Range.
