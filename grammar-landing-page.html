02 / A necessary distinction

Category is the shelf.
Function is the gesture.

Category

A word’s familiar family: noun, verb, adjective. Useful for orientation, never enough for interpretation.

Function

The live job in a phrase or clause: subject, modifier, complement, connector, stance marker.

05 / Beyond labels

Semantics says what.
Pragmatics asks why.

Semantics is the stable contribution a form makes to meaning. Pragmatics is the live negotiation: what a writer assumes, softens, foregrounds or leaves for the reader to infer.

Compare: “The evidence is limited” and “The evidence is only limited.” A small focus word changes the social action.

Reader’s question
What does this choice make easier to believe, and what does it ask the reader to supply?
