Elementary English Level Test

Four modules, one report. Read, listen, write and speak, then get your score for each module, your CEFR band and clear advice on what to practise next.

Reading

Signs, messages, a timetable, an email and short articles.

40 questions · about 60 min

Listening

British, Australian and Canadian speakers in everyday situations.

26 questions · about 35 min

Writing

An email and a short story, each scored with a strict four-part rubric.

2 tasks · about 60 min

Speaking

Choose a topic, record your answers, listen back and check yourself.

2 recordings · about 20 min

Before you start

  • You have 2 hours 55 minutes in total: Reading 60 min, Listening 35 min, Writing 60 min and Speaking 20 min.
  • Each module has its own timer. It starts when you press Start the test and runs only while you are working in that module. It pauses when you go to another module and stops when you leave or close this page. If you refresh or come back later, each timer carries on from the time already used (your answers are kept).
  • To start the whole test again, use Reset and retake the test at the bottom of the page.
  • When a module’s time is over you will see a reminder. The test does not close, so you can still finish.
  • You can move between modules in any order. Your answers are saved in this browser.
  • You can play each listening recording twice. Use headphones if you can.
  • The Speaking module needs a microphone. Your recordings stay on this device and are not uploaded.
  • You can submit only when all four modules are complete: every question answered, both writing tasks scored and the speaking tasks done.
Module 1

Reading

Nine parts, 40 questions. Read the texts carefully and answer every question.

Answer all 40 questions to complete Reading.

Module 2

Listening

Six parts, 26 questions. Press play to hear each recording. You can play each one twice, and you can pause.

Answer all 26 questions to complete Listening.

Module 3

Writing

Two tasks. When you submit a task, it is scored at once with the rubric below. You cannot change a task after you submit it.

Submit both tasks to complete Writing.

Module 4

Speaking

Choose one topic. Listen to the examiner, record your answers and play them back. Then complete the self-check.

When every module is complete, submit the test to see your report.

Want to start again?

Reset deletes all your answers, scores and recordings, and every timer starts again from the beginning.
